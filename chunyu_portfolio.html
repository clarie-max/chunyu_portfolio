About Me

Game & Interaction Designer

I'm Chunyu Ji, an MFA student in Interactive Media & Games at the University of Southern California (USC), pursuing opportunities in game design and level design. I turn research and abstract ideas into systems that players can understand through action, space, feedback, and choice.

My projects explore core loops, narrative interaction, progression, spatial guidance, and multimodal feedback. I enjoy moving from concepts and flow diagrams to playable Unity prototypes, then testing and iterating with real users.

With an undergraduate background in Industrial Design from South China University of Technology, I connect player experience with implementation. I bring visual communication, systems thinking, user research, and hands-on development to collaborative game teams.

Shattered Light

Graduation Project · 2026

Role: Solo Game Designer, Level Designer, Researcher & Unity Developer

A multimodal narrative puzzle game for university students experiencing academic stress. Three connected levels translate CBT strategies—behavioral activation, cognitive restructuring, and problem-solving—into exploration, dialogue choices, tasks, and environmental feedback.

Design contribution: Defined the experience, structured the three-level emotional arc, designed interaction and feedback systems, built the Unity prototype, and planned user testing.

Result: Testing with 13 students achieved an SUS score of 80.8 and a PGQ score of 89.7. CBT knowledge accuracy increased from 48.5% to 88.7%.
